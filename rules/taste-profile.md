# Taste Profile

These rules are the primary signal. Review sentiment is useful only after the game has been judged against this profile.

## Prefer Signals

| Signal | Weight | Keywords | Why |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| skill-based difficulty | 4 | skill-based, precision, challenging, difficult, mastery, parry, dodge, timing, boss, soulslike, roguelite, character action, spectacle fighter | Difficulty is welcome when it asks for execution and learning rather than patience. |
| tight combat | 5 | tight combat, responsive, melee, action, combat, shooter, fps, hack and slash, stylish action, twin-stick, boomer shooter | Combat feel can create immediate pull and justify attention. |
| strong pacing | 4 | paced, short, concise, linear, mission-based, campaign, focused, no filler, brisk, arcade | A game should respect limited attention and move with intent. |
| clear design intent | 3 | focused, authored, curated, handcrafted, puzzle, level design, immersive sim, metroidvania, tactical | Clear intent beats broad-but-mushy feature spread. |
| low maintenance friction | 3 | low grind, no grind, streamlined, pick-up-and-play, autosave, checkpoints, accessible | Low overhead makes it easier to return and finish. |
| high mechanical payoff | 5 | buildcraft, combo, movement, traversal, mechanics, systems, sandbox, emergent, physics, deckbuilder | The game should pay back attention with satisfying play. |
| creates pull quickly | 4 | immediate, addictive, compelling, hook, strong opening, fast start, loop, momentum | Early pull matters because the goal is clarity, not obligation. |

## Penalize Signals

| Signal | Weight | Keywords | Why |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| grind gates | -5 | grind, grindy, farming, level gate, gear score, xp gate, daily, battle pass, live service | Repetition used as gating turns play into maintenance. |
| durability or resource maintenance | -4 | durability, weapon break, hunger, thirst, survival meter, crafting upkeep, repairs, inventory management | Maintenance loops often compete with the interesting part of play. |
| vague progression | -4 | vague, aimless, opaque, unclear, open-ended, directionless, sandbox only, checklist | Unclear progress makes attention harder to justify. |
| worklike strategy management | -4 | management, grand strategy, 4x, colony sim, spreadsheet, logistics, automation, supply chain, city builder | Strategic overhead is penalized when it feels like work. |
| jank without upside | -4 | janky, clunky, buggy, eurojank, rough, awkward controls, unstable | Rough edges need a strong compensating payoff. |
| unfinished series friction | -5 | sequel, trilogy, episode, part 2, part ii, sequel-first | Sequels lose urgency when prior entries are unfinished. |
| competent but replaceable | -3 | generic, average, competent, formulaic, ubisoft-like, open world, collectathon, derivative | A limited-attention backlog needs more than adequacy. |

# Category Definitions

## Shortlist

Owned games that are active finish candidates. These should have strong personal-fit evidence, low enough friction, and a clear reason to compete now.

## Yes: Later

Owned games with validated future interest. They do not need immediate play, but they should remain in the library as credible future candidates.

## Sample

Owned games where uncertainty is worth testing. The next action is a limited sample, not a commitment to finish.

## DNF

Owned games that were tested and rejected. Use this when play experience has provided enough evidence that continuing is not a good use of attention.

## No

Games that are not competitive for limited attention. This can include owned games, wishlist games, and games rejected because they are merely competent but replaceable.

`No` has precedence over computed `Unplayed`. A game with zero playtime should still be excluded from unplayed review if it has already been intentionally ruled out.

## Wishlist Hold

Wishlist games that should stay unbought until there is stronger evidence that they would become `Shortlist` or `Yes: Later`.

## Buy Soon

Wishlist games with strong personal-fit evidence and low purchase-risk. These are likely to compete for attention soon after purchase.
